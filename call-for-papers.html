INTERLATTICE: Journal of Emerging Technologies in Physical and Computational Sciences

ISSN (Online / Print): Pending | Publication Frequency: Quad-annual (January, April, July, October)

The Department of Physics and Department of Information Technology at the University of Mumbai invite researchers, academics, and industry experts to submit high-quality original manuscripts for upcoming issues of INTERLATTICE.

INTERLATTICE is a premier, double-blind peer-reviewed, open-access journal dedicated to publishing state-of-the-art research at the convergence of modern physics, artificial intelligence, and advanced computational techniques.

1. Subject Areas & Expected Scope

Computational Physics & Modelling

  • Quantum algorithms and numerical simulations
  • Condensed matter physics and materials modeling
  • Computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and astrophysics simulations
  • Statistical mechanics and non-linear dynamics

Physics-Informed AI & Data Science

  • Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs)
  • AI-driven discovery and modeling of smart materials
  • Computer vision and pattern recognition for observational physics
  • Natural language processing for scientific literature discovery

Advanced & Emerging Technologies

  • High-performance computing (HPC) and parallel algorithms
  • Quantum computing architectures and software stacks
  • Cloud-edge architectures for scientific computing
  • Cybersecurity and data integrity in scientific research infrastructure

Applied & Interdisciplinary Frontiers

  • Medical physics and computational bio-imaging
  • AI-driven robotics and bio-computing
  • Dynamic physical modeling and bio-hydrodynamics

2. Accepted Manuscript Types

  • Original Research Articles: Full-length empirical, computational, or theoretical studies presenting novel methodology or discoveries.
  • Review Articles & Surveys: Comprehensive overviews of emerging trends, benchmark analyses, or state-of-the-art developments.
  • Short Communications & Technical Letters: Rapid communications detailing timely computational breakthroughs, novel software tools, or preliminary findings.

3. Submission Guidelines & Policy

  • Rolling Submissions: Submissions are accepted year-round on a continuous rolling basis for upcoming quarterly issues.
  • No Article Processing Charges (APCs): To promote global research contribution, all accepted manuscripts during the initial 3 years will be published free of charge.
  • Peer Review Process: Double-blind evaluation by a minimum of two independent reviewers.
  • Plagiarism Threshold: A maximum similarity index of 10% will be strictly enforced prior to review.
