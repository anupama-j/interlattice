Author Guidelines & Submission

Manuscript Preparation

  • Manuscripts must be original, in English, and not under consideration elsewhere.
  • Include title, abstract (150–250 words), 4–6 keywords, and structured sections (Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion).
  • Submit as .docx or LaTeX with a PDF; figures at 300 dpi or vector format.
  • Use IEEE-style numbered references; equations numbered consecutively.
  • Share datasets and code via a repository (e.g., Zenodo, GitHub) and cite them in a Data Availability Statement.

Review Process

  1. Initial screening by editorial office, including similarity check (below 15%).
  2. Double-blind peer review by at least two independent reviewers.
  3. Decision: accept, minor revision, major revision or reject, typically within 6–8 weeks.
  4. Production and online publication, with a DOI assigned.

Fees & Copyright

There are no submission or article-processing charges. Authors retain copyright; articles are licensed under CC BY 4.0.
