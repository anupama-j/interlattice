Aim, Objectives & Scope

Aim

INTERLATTICE aims to provide a rigorous, open and interdisciplinary platform where physical sciences and computational sciences meet. The journal publishes original research that advances, applies and critically examines emerging technologies, and encourages work that interweaves theory, experiment, simulation and data-driven methods.

Objectives

  1. Promote high-quality original research at the intersection of physical and computational sciences.
  2. Provide a visible international forum for scholars, scientists and industry researchers, with special encouragement to early-career authors.
  3. Foster interdisciplinary collaboration among physics, computer science, mathematics, materials science and engineering.
  4. Encourage reproducible research through open data, shared code and transparent methodology.
  5. Disseminate emerging-technology knowledge through open-access publication, free of article-processing charges.
  6. Uphold the highest standards of peer review, publication ethics and academic integrity.
  7. Support translation of research outcomes into societal, industrial and educational benefit in line with national research priorities.

Scope

The journal covers theoretical, computational and experimental work, including but not limited to:

  • Quantum Technologies
  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
  • Computational Physics
  • Materials & Nanoscience
  • Photonics & Optoelectronics
  • High-Performance Computing
  • Data Science & Analytics
  • Complex Systems & Network Science
  • Semiconductor & Device Physics
  • Scientific Software & Simulation
  • Internet of Things & Sensors
  • Cybersecurity & Cryptography

Article Types

Research Article

Original, complete investigations (6,000–9,000 words).

Review Article

Critical, up-to-date reviews of a developing field (up to 12,000 words).

Short Communication

Concise, significant preliminary findings (2,000–3,500 words).

Technical Note

Software, datasets, instruments and methods.
