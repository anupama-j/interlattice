Where physical science meets computation.

INTERLATTICE aims to provide a rigorous, open and interdisciplinary platform where physical sciences and computational sciences meet. The journal publishes original research that advances, applies and critically examines emerging technologies, and encourages work that interweaves theory, experiment, simulation and data-driven methods.

About the Journal

INTERLATTICE: Journal of Emerging Technologies in Physical and Computational Sciences is a peer-reviewed, open-access international research journal published by the University of Mumbai. It welcomes original research articles, review articles, short communications and technical notes from academia and industry worldwide.

The name Interlattice reflects the journal's vision: a lattice of ideas in which physics, mathematics, computer science and engineering are interconnected, and in which each discipline strengthens the others.
